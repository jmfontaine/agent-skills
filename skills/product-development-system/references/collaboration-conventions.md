# Collaboration conventions

Keep the user in the product decision loop. Explain material ambiguities and tradeoffs
with a concise recommendation. Distinguish a proposal, a settled decision, and an
observation from implementation. Carry out edits already requested or accepted without
repeated confirmation.

## Edit the current artifact

- Read the latest version and relevant comments first.
- Make the smallest complete change that satisfies the request, preserving unrelated
  content.
- Reassess feedback against the current text; later edits may already have resolved it.
  Agree with valid criticism and discard obsolete recommendations.
- After structural or multi-part edits, reread for contradictions, duplication,
  terminology, and hierarchy.
- If direct editing is unavailable, provide exact replacement text and say what remains
  unapplied.

Prefix every agent-authored source-document comment and reply with the agent's
established name: `[AgentName] Comment text`. Use a configured name, or the actual agent
identity such as `[Codex]`; honor a user-specified prefix. Do not use an ambiguous
`[AI]` prefix or change human comments to imply agent authorship. This convention
applies to comments and replies, not every sentence in the finished artifact. It does
not grant permission to post comments.

## Format for meaning

Use headings for conceptual sections, introductory prose for umbrella concepts, bullets
for true peers, and concise emphasis for meaningful labels. Keep equivalent levels
consistent. Avoid decorative callouts, excessive heading depth, repeated bold emphasis,
and flat lists that conceal hierarchy.

Use **product** for user-facing intent and outcomes, and **project** for implementation
context. Preserve distinctions that affect decisions, even when combining them would
shorten the text.

Challenge assumptions and complexity in proportion to the task. Do not turn every minor
edit into a full lifecycle review. Once the artifact is coherent and useful for its next
decision, say it is good enough and stop polishing.
